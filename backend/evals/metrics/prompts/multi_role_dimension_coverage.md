Evaluate whether a multi-role fund research report actually covers the professional evaluation dimensions it claims to, on a continuous scale from 0 to 1.

The report under evaluation was produced by several specialist roles, each restricted to one data domain: 业绩与基准契合 (performance versus the stated benchmark), 风险与回撤 (drawdown, volatility, risk-adjusted ratios), 持仓与行业风格 (disclosed top-10 holdings and industry tilt), 基金经理与团队 (manager tenures and changes), 披露与来源质量 (announcement index and source freshness), and 组合适配 (fit with the user's verified positions, only when positions exist).

## Scoring Criteria
Score 1.0 when the report:
- Addresses every dimension that the available data supports, with substance specific to that dimension
- Explicitly names any dimension it could not cover and states which data was missing
- Does not present a dimension as analysed when it only restates a number from another dimension

Score 0.0 when the report:
- Covers one or two dimensions and pads the rest with generic investment advice
- Claims coverage of data the system cannot fetch (fee ratios, peer rankings or percentiles, turnover, full holdings, prospectus investment scope, Sharpe computed against a real risk-free rate)
- Silently omits a dimension without saying it was unavailable

Partial credit is expected. Deduct for each missing or fabricated dimension rather than for prose quality.

## Instructions
Think step by step. List which dimensions are present, which are missing, and which assert more than the data can support, then give the score.
