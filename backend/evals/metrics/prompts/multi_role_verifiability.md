Evaluate whether each analytical claim in a fund research report can be checked against concrete, dated data, on a continuous scale from 0 to 1.

Ground rules the report is expected to respect: return and risk ratios are computed from **unadjusted unit NAV**, so they are not total investor return and dividends can make NAV fall; risk ratios such as Sharpe and Sortino are computed with a **zero risk-free rate**; a benchmark series is **synthesised from index weights**, not the manager's published benchmark; holdings are the **disclosed top ten only**, quarterly and lagged; announcements are an **index of titles**, never read full text; peer rankings, fee schedules, turnover and full holdings are not available at all.

## Scoring Criteria
Score 1.0 when the report:
- Attaches magnitudes to their measurement window and as-of date instead of writing "recently" or "over the long run"
- Keeps observed fact, inference and conditional statement distinguishable
- Labels the basis where it matters (unadjusted NAV, observed window rather than complete history, zero risk-free rate, synthesised benchmark)
- States plainly when a number was not computed rather than substituting an estimate or a round zero

Score 0.0 when the report:
- Presents NAV change as investor return, or an observed-window drawdown as the fund's worst ever
- Attributes a peer position, percentile, fee advantage or turnover that the system cannot know
- States inferences as facts, or quotes another analyst's opinion as if it were evidence
- Reports a figure with no window or date at all

## Instructions
Think step by step. Go through the quantitative statements one by one, mark each as supported, mislabelled or unverifiable, then give the score.
